About Me

Hi, I'm Sean.

I'm a developer, designer, and overall creative. I've never been a fan of labels. More often than not, I'm whatever the situation needs me to be. If I had to pick one title, it would probably be solutionist.

Based in Dallas, Texas, I care deeply about solving real problems and building things that make people's lives better. Just as importantly, I believe those solutions should look and feel as good as they work.

My background spans software development, UX research, product management, design, and 3D visualization. I enjoy working across disciplines and connecting technical thinking with creative execution.

When I'm not building, you'll probably find me consuming art, exploring new ideas, bringing side projects to life, or doomscrolling when I should be doing literally anything else.
